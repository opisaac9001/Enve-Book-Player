# Reading recaps

Journal → Recaps shows one calendar month or year at a time. It opens on the current month. Month and Year chips switch the scope, and the chevrons step one period at a time. Next is disabled once a period contains the present, so a future period is never shown. Previous stops at the period holding the profile's earliest recorded session; empty periods in between remain reachable and show an empty state. Changing scope keeps the latest visible moment: the current year opens the current month, and a past year opens its December. Insights links to the same screen. It does not keep a second copy of the annual layout.

`JournalInsightsPolicy.recap` and the existing annual review use the same activity computation. The yearly review in Insights keeps its API. Sessions are deduplicated by ID. Sessions with nonpositive duration or an end time after now are dropped. Each session belongs to the period and day containing its end time in the calendar and time zone captured when the screen opens. Periods start at their calendar interval and end one instant before the next interval begins, so a session ending exactly at midnight on the 1st belongs to the new month. Listening counts `audiobook` and `podcast` sessions and reading counts `ebook` sessions. The total is all sessions. Pages are the sum of recorded `pagesRead`. Top book, author, and narrator are the leaders by time. Ties sort by title and then stable ID, or by name; the busiest-day tie goes to the earlier day. Leaders only include sessions whose book resolves through `JournalStatsBookLookup` for the loaded session IDs. The full catalog is never fetched.

Recaps leave out finished-book counts. `FinishedBookSummary.lastUpdate` records the last change to the book, not when it was finished, so it cannot place a completion in a month. The screen says this. Recaps also only see the history kept on the device: `HistorySessionStore` trims each ledger to its most recent sessions, so the earliest available months can be incomplete. The footnote explains this. No past data is synthesized.

`JournalRecapModel` captures the `ProfileSession` history store, book store, and progress cache when it is created. Overlapping refreshes keep only the newest result.

Sharing uses `ShareLink`. Users can share the plain-text summary shown in the Share card, or a fixed-size image of the same totals. The image is rendered locally with `ImageRenderer` from `JournalRecapCard`. Each share preview names the period and says whether it holds text or an image. Book titles and creator names are included. Covers are not loaded or embedded. Nothing is sent until the user chooses a destination in the system share sheet.

Apple references: [Calendar.dateInterval(of:for:)](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/calendar/dateinterval(of:for:)), [ShareLink](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/sharelink), [SharePreview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/sharepreview), and [ImageRenderer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/imagerenderer).
