# Community Code of Conduct

The Enve community should be a useful and welcoming place for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and discuss the work rather than the person.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Make room for questions and honest mistakes.
- Respect privacy and never pressure someone to share credentials, private server details, or personal media.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope without harassment or repeated disruption.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting a problem

Report conduct concerns privately to `enve.audiobook@gmail.com`. Include links or screenshots when useful. Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical, and maintainers may edit or remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
